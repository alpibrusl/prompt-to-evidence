Prompt to Evidence

The data literacy your AI agent assumes you already know

For people who are not experts in math or statistics but need to make decisions from data — in economics, science, marketing, wherever an agent can now produce a number on request. The reader works comfortably in Excel and wants to go where their data team already lives, not become a statistician.
